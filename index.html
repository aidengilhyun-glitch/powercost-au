How the calculation works

Cost = watts ÷ 1,000 × hours used × electricity rate. We convert watts to kilowatts, multiply by usage time, then apply your tariff. Monthly figures use 365 ÷ 12 days and account for the number of days used each week.

Finding your electricity rate

Look for the usage charge on your electricity bill, shown in cents per kilowatt-hour (c/kWh). If you are on a time-of-use tariff, calculate peak and off-peak usage separately for a more accurate estimate.

Important

Results are general estimates, not financial or energy advice. Actual consumption can vary with appliance efficiency, settings, temperature, standby use and cycling.
