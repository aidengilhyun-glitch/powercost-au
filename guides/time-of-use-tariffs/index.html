Peak, Shoulder and Off-Peak Electricity Tariffs

Learn how time-of-use electricity prices affect appliance running-cost calculations.

What is a time-of-use tariff?

A time-of-use plan applies different usage rates at different times. The exact periods and prices depend on your retailer, network and plan.

Calculate each period separately

If an appliance runs for one hour at a peak rate and two hours off-peak, calculate those portions separately and add the results.

Controlled loads

Some hot-water or heating circuits may use a separately metered controlled-load rate. Confirm the applicable circuit and tariff on your bill before calculating.

Do not assume off-peak is always cheaper overall

Compare the whole plan, including supply charges and all usage periods. Moving one appliance does not prove that a different retail plan will reduce the total bill.

Use the result carefully

These explanations provide general information. Check your current bill, plan documents and appliance information. PowerCost AU does not provide energy-plan or financial advice.
