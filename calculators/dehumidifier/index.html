Understanding dehumidifier electricity use

Consumption depends on capacity, humidity target, room temperature and how often the compressor cycles.

How to improve accuracy

Use the input watts from your appliance label, manual or energy monitor. Enter the usage charge from your bill in cents per kilowatt-hour. For time-of-use plans, calculate peak and off-peak periods separately.

Ways to reduce the cost

Close doors and windows, clean the filter and select a realistic humidity target.

Formula

Watts ÷ 1,000 × hours × tariff = electricity cost. Results are general estimates and actual consumption varies.
