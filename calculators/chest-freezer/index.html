Understanding chest freezer electricity use

A freezer compressor cycles rather than running continuously. Ambient temperature, seals, frost and loading affect its annual energy use.

Improve the estimate

Use electrical input watts from the label or manual and the usage charge shown on your electricity bill. For cycling appliances, a measured average or annual energy label is usually better than maximum watts.

Ways to reduce the cost

Keep seals clean, defrost excessive ice and place the unit in a ventilated area away from heat sources.
