⚡ PowerCost AU

ABOUT

Clear electricity estimates, without the sales pitch.

PowerCost AU is an independent calculator project designed to help Australian households understand appliance running costs.

Our method

Calculators apply the standard relationship between appliance power, usage time and the electricity usage rate entered by the visitor. Typical values are starting points only; the appliance label, energy label or an energy monitor is more accurate.

Editorial principles

We separate estimates from facts, explain assumptions and avoid promising bill savings. Future advertising or affiliate relationships will be clearly disclosed and will not change calculator results.

Contact
