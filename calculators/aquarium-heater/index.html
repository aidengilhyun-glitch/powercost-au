Understanding aquarium heater electricity use

Aquarium heaters cycle according to tank size, target temperature and room conditions. The rated watts are not usually continuous draw.

Improve the estimate

Use electrical input watts from the label or manual and the usage charge shown on your electricity bill. For cycling appliances, a measured average or annual energy label is usually better than maximum watts.

Ways to reduce the cost

Use a suitable heater and thermostat, cover the tank where appropriate and avoid placing it in cold draughts.
