How to compare fairly

Compare appliances that deliver a similar outcome and use realistic runtimes. Rated watts represent maximum or nominal input and may not equal average consumption for thermostatic or variable-speed appliances.

What is excluded?

Results exclude daily supply charges, discounts, demand tariffs, maintenance, purchase price and differences in performance. They are estimates, not recommendations to replace a working appliance.
