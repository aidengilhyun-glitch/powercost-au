Understanding microwave oven electricity use

Input wattage can be higher than the advertised cooking output. Check the rating label for the electrical input figure.

How to improve accuracy

Use the electrical input watts from the appliance label, manual or an energy monitor. Enter the usage charge from your electricity bill. Calculate peak and off-peak periods separately if applicable.

Ways to reduce the cost

Use suitable containers, cover food and choose the shortest effective cooking time.

Calculation formula

Watts ÷ 1,000 × hours × tariff = electricity cost. Actual consumption varies by model, settings and operating conditions.
