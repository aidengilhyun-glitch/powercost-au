Understanding electric oven electricity use

An oven cycles its heating elements after reaching temperature, so the rated wattage is a useful upper starting point rather than constant draw.

How to improve accuracy

Use the electrical input watts from the appliance label, manual or an energy monitor. Enter the usage charge from your electricity bill. Calculate peak and off-peak periods separately if applicable.

Ways to reduce the cost

Cook multiple items together, avoid opening the door repeatedly and use smaller appliances for small portions.

Calculation formula

Watts ÷ 1,000 × hours × tariff = electricity cost. Actual consumption varies by model, settings and operating conditions.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a electric oven cost to run?

The result depends on input watts, hours used and your electricity tariff. Enter the figures above to calculate an estimate for your household.

Is the default wattage exact?

No. It is a general starting point. Use the rating label, energy label or a plug-in energy monitor for a more useful estimate.

Does the result include the daily supply charge?

No. The calculator estimates appliance usage charges only. Your electricity bill may also include supply charges, demand charges, taxes and discounts.
