Contact and editorial policy

PowerCost AU is an independent educational calculator site for Australian households. This page explains how our estimates are produced, how to report a problem, and the standards we follow when publishing or updating content.

How to contact us

For a calculation error, broken link, accessibility problem, or suggested appliance, please open a public issue in our project repository. Include the page address, the values you entered, the result you expected, and the result shown.

Report an issue on GitHub

Please do not include account numbers, bills, addresses, phone numbers, or other personal information. PowerCost AU does not need those details to investigate a calculator.

Calculation methodology

Our standard estimate uses the formula: appliance power in kilowatts multiplied by hours of use multiplied by the electricity usage rate in dollars per kilowatt-hour. Daily estimates are multiplied by the selected number of days for monthly or annual figures.

Example: a 1,500 watt heater used for 3 hours at $0.30 per kWh is estimated to cost 1.5 x 3 x $0.30 = $1.35 for that day.

Results are estimates, not bills or quotes. Real costs can differ because appliances cycle on and off, tariffs may vary by time of day, plans may include supply charges, solar exports can offset usage, and device labels may show maximum rather than typical power.

Editorial standards

  • Calculator formulas must be visible or clearly explained.
  • Australian spelling, dollars, units and household context are used.
  • Claims that may change are checked against current authoritative sources where practical.
  • Advertising does not determine calculator results or editorial conclusions.
  • Pages are updated when a material error, outdated assumption or broken function is identified.

Corrections and review

When a report is reproducible, we correct the affected formula or explanation and publish the change through the site's public version history. Minor wording and accessibility improvements may be made without a separate notice.

We review popular calculators and guides periodically. The date shown in the public repository history is the authoritative record of the latest change.

Independence, advertising and affiliate disclosure

PowerCost AU may display advertising to support hosting and maintenance. Ads are labelled or delivered by the advertising provider and do not change the numbers calculated by our tools.

If we add affiliate links in future, they will be identified near the relevant link. A commission would not increase the visitor's price. We do not accept payment to alter calculator outputs.

Important disclaimer

The site provides general information only. It is not financial, electrical, engineering, safety, legal, or energy-retail advice. Check your appliance label, electricity plan and bill, and use a qualified professional where safety or installation is involved.
