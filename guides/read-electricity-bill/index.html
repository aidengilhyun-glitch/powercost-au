How to Read an Australian Electricity Bill

Find the usage rate, supply charge and other figures needed for accurate electricity cost estimates.

Usage charge

The usage charge is normally shown in cents per kilowatt-hour (c/kWh). This is the figure to enter into a PowerCost AU calculator.

Daily supply charge

The supply charge is a fixed daily amount for connection to the network. Appliance calculators exclude it because using one appliance does not usually change that fixed charge.

Other tariff structures

Your bill may show separate peak, shoulder, off-peak, controlled-load or demand charges. Use the rate that applies when the appliance operates, and calculate different periods separately if necessary.

Discounts and taxes

Retailer discounts, concessions, credits and taxes can change the final bill. Calculator results are usage estimates, not predictions of the final amount payable.

Use the result carefully

These explanations provide general information. Check your current bill, plan documents and appliance information. PowerCost AU does not provide energy-plan or financial advice.
