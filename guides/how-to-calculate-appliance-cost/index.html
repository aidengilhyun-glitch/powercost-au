How to Calculate Appliance Electricity Cost

Learn the simple formula for estimating appliance electricity costs in Australia using watts, hours and your usage tariff.

The basic formula

Watts ÷ 1,000 × hours used × electricity rate in dollars per kWh = running cost.

Dividing watts by 1,000 converts power to kilowatts. Multiplying by hours gives kilowatt-hours (kWh), the unit commonly used for electricity billing.

Worked example

A 2,000-watt heater used for three hours consumes an estimated 6 kWh. At 32 cents per kWh, the usage charge is about $1.92 for that session.

Why actual use can differ

Thermostats, compressors, variable-speed motors, standby modes and environmental conditions mean many appliances do not draw their rated power continuously. Use an energy label or plug-in meter when available.
